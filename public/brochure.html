Origin Narrative

Where Our Flavor Stories Begin

Our portfolio is intentionally compact: four origins, each with distinct sensory character, harvest rhythm, and menu utility. This keeps your coffee program focused, premium, and easy to communicate to customers.

Ethiopia

Yirgacheffe & Harrar | 1,800-2,200m | Harvest: Oct-Jan

The birthplace of coffee. Floral aromatics, citrus clarity, and deeply expressive natural profiles. Ethiopia anchors your high-acidity and specialty-forward offerings.

Colombia

Huila & Nariño | 1,600-2,200m | Dual Harvest Cycles

Structured sweetness and bright precision. From elegant washed cups to exotic high-end micro-lots, Colombia supports both everyday premium menus and flagship reserve offerings.

Brazil

Minas Gerais & Chapada Diamantina | 900-1,400m

Foundational body and chocolate depth, ideal for espresso and milk-based programs. Our Brazilian lots balance consistency with modern fermentation innovation.

India

Coorg, Wayanad & Araku | 900-1,700m | Harvest: Nov-Feb

Spiced complexity, earthy sweetness, and strong local identity. Indian coffees give your menu regional relevance while maintaining specialty quality standards.
